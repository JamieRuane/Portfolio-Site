About me

I'm Jamie, a student at the University of Limerick who likes building things people actually use, from neon arcade racers to courses that teach nine programming languages.

I care about the details that make software feel good: procedurally generated audio in a racing game, exercises that are checked against a real compiler, a CLI with zero dependencies. Most of what I build starts as something I wanted to exist, and I keep going until it's polished enough to share.

05

Beyond the classroom

Volunteering, societies, mentoring and leadership.

volunteering · fundraising

Hope Foundation ambassador

I raised over €2,500 for the Hope Foundation, then travelled to Kolkata, India in March 2024 as one of its ambassadors to see the work the money supports.

My contribution
Fundraising in my own community ahead of the trip, then representing the foundation in Kolkata as part of a large ambassador group, visiting its projects and sharing what I saw with people back home.
Impact on others
The money went directly to the foundation's work with children and families in Kolkata. Telling people at home about it afterwards also helped build awareness and support beyond the trip itself.
Impact on my own development
Seeing the work first-hand put my own circumstances in perspective. It showed me how much difference organised, sustained effort can make, and made me want my own work to have a real effect on people.
Skills I gained
  • Fundraising
  • Communication
  • Adaptability
  • Teamwork
  • Cultural awareness
  • Resilience
leadership · representation

Student council member

I was elected by my peers to the student council at Coláiste Mhuire Askeaton, representing students' views to staff and helping to run school initiatives.

My contribution
Bringing students' ideas and concerns to council meetings and to staff, and helping to plan and run initiatives for the school, including work around positive mental health.
Impact on others
Students had a voice in decisions that affected them, and ideas raised by students could be turned into real changes around the school.
Impact on my own development
Being elected showed me that my peers trusted me to speak for them. I learned to listen properly, represent views that weren't always my own, and work with staff to find solutions that suited everyone.
Skills I gained
  • Leadership
  • Active listening
  • Representing others
  • Communication
  • Compromise
  • Organisation
