About

I'm Jamie, a student at the University of Limerick who likes building things people actually use, from neon arcade racers to courses that teach nine programming languages.

I care about the details that make software feel good: procedurally generated audio in a racing game, exercises that are checked against a real compiler, a CLI with zero dependencies. Most of what I build starts as something I wanted to exist, and I keep going until it's polished enough to share.
